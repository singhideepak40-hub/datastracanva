# 🎬 Sudarshan Studio: 60-Second Live Demonstration Script

### Concept Target
Show how the system's **behaviour expresses an idea**: shifting instantly from cold, slow, reflective melancholy to warm, fast, vibrant, golden joy.

---

### Phase 1: Idle Breathing Equilibrium (0:00 - 0:10)
- **Visual:** Open app. The dark obsidian Sudarshan studio UI displays the live p5.js canvas undulating in a soft sinusoidal purple/blue harmonic drift.
- **Presenter:** *"Notice how Sudarshan Studio feels alive before any input. The canvas is breathing in equilibrium, waiting to mirror our emotional pulse."*

---

### Phase 2: Melancholic & Poetic Input (0:10 - 0:30)
- **Action:** Select **📝 Express Emotion (Text)**.
- **Input (Hinglish/English):** *"Bohot akela lag raha hai, purani yaadein aur thandi baarish dil ko udaas kar rahi hai."*
- **Click:** **🔮 Breathe Life into Canvas**.
- **System Behaviour Response:**
  - **HUD Badge:** Changes to `● SADNESS STATE (Valence: -0.75 | Arousal: 0.25)`.
  - **Live Canvas:** Particles immediately decelerate into a slow, cold indigo-cyan weeping drift ($0.35\times$ speed).
  - **Center View:** Scene visual updates to *Babu the Clockwork Sparrow* gazing out of a misty windowpane streaked with raindrops in deep midnight blue.
  - **Audio:** Web Audio switches to soft filtered rain noise.
  - **Story Lore:** Reads a tender chapter in Hinglish about memories lingering in the twilight.
  - **Timeline:** Plotly graph drops into the deep blue negative quadrant.

---

### Phase 3: The Joyful Transformation (0:30 - 0:50)
- **Action:** In the right column under **🔀 Steer the Next Scene**, click:
  *"एक नन्हा दिया जलाकर रौशनी की तलाश करना"* (or type *"Suddenly sunlight bursts through! Pure joy and golden laughter everywhere!"*).
- **System Behaviour Response:**
  - **HUD Badge:** Flares into `● JOY STATE (Valence: +0.85 | Arousal: 0.75)`.
  - **Live Canvas:** Particles immediately ignite with high-speed golden and amber swirling starlight ($1.8\times$ speed, radiant halo).
  - **Center View:** Visual transforms via Ken Burns zoom into a sun-drenched attic bursting with floating stardust, glowing gears, and Pixar-style golden hour bokeh.
  - **Audio:** Major celestial chimes synthesize. TTS narration reads with upbeat cadence.
  - **Timeline:** The trajectory line leaps sharply upwards into the high positive valence/arousal quadrant.

---

### Phase 4: Export & Collectible Memory (0:50 - 1:00)
- **Action:** Click **💾 Save Session** to archive into SQLite Memory Wall, then click **📄 Export PDF**.
- **Presenter:** *"In 60 seconds, our canvas transformed colour, motion, pacing, and narrative to reflect the human heart. That is Sudarshan Studio."*
